Add `gigadrive microvms` (alias `gigadrive vm`): `create`, `ls`, `get`, `exec <id> -- <command>` (streams output and exits with the command's exit code), `shell <id>` (interactive terminal), `cp` between local paths and `vm:<id>:<path>`, `stop`, `rm`, `extend` and `images ls|create|rm`. Login now requests the microVM scopes.
